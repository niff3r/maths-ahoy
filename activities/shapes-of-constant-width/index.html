Shapes of constant width

Is a circle the only shape that rolls smoothly between two flat surfaces?

What students do

Students go hunting for shapes that are the same width whichever way you measure them. A circle works, but it isn't the only one. The Reuleaux triangle, a triangle with curved sides, has the same property.

Students construct their own Reuleaux triangles in Desmos, laser cut them, then put them to the test. Does it roll as smoothly as a circle? Can it turn inside a square frame while touching all four sides?

The maths behind it

A shape of constant width measures the same across in every direction. The Reuleaux triangle is built from arcs of three overlapping circles, and it turns up in surprising places, from drill bits that cut almost-square holes to the UK's 20p and 50p coins, which are curved seven-sided shapes of constant width.
