Activities / The birthday trick

The birthday trick

Tell me which of these five cards show the day of the month you were born, and I'll name the day.

What students do

Someone looks at five cards covered in numbers and says yes or no: is the day of the month they were born on this card? From their answers alone, the day comes out, every time. Five cards can find any number from 1 to 31, which covers every day of every month.

The secret is in the corners. Each card has 1, 2, 4, 8, or 16 in its top corner, and adding up the corner numbers of the “yes” cards gives the day. Students work out why that works, then try the trick on someone new.

The challenge: build your own deck

Then the cards are put away. Can students rebuild the whole set from scratch, without the originals in front of them? To decide which numbers belong on each card, they have to break every number from 1 to 31 into powers of 2. Copying a pattern won't get them there. They need to really understand how numbers are built in binary.

The maths behind it

Every whole number can be written in exactly one way as a sum of different powers of 2. That's binary, the number system computers use. Each card collects the numbers that need its power of 2, so the yes and no answers spell out the day in binary.
