Activities

I love designing hands-on maths tasks with multiple pathways in, the kind that need a bit of perseverance and a lot of sharing of ideas. They take all sorts of forms: puzzles, art, games, and the occasional magic trick. Here are a few favourites.

Crazy cubes

A deceptively tricky cube puzzle, solved with network graphs.

Resurrecting the deck

How many perfect shuffles until a deck of cards comes back in order?

Spirographs

How many petals will the flower have? It depends on the teeth.

Stationery maths

Make paper clips and rubber bands do some strange things on strips of paper.

Dizzying squares

Doodle infinite squares to see how an infinite set of numbers can add up.

The birthday trick

Guess the day of anyone’s birthday, from 1 to 31, with five cards and some powers of 2.

Shapes of constant width

Find shapes that roll as smoothly as a circle, then laser cut your own.

Spirolaterals

Walk, turn, repeat. Does the pen ever get back home?

The lazy Susan roller coaster

Build a matchstick roller coaster, then give it a safety rating.
